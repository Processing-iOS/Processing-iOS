Processing 3.0 is completely free for everyone! All features are included, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Sponsored by one sec: https://one-sec.app

A better place to create
• Enjoy sharper Retina drawing and a simpler app name: Processing.
• Add a front or back camera to your sketches with CameraView, and explore more device sensors.
• Save your drawings as PNG or JPEG images.

More comfortable coding
• Write with improved syntax highlighting, code completion, find and replace, undo and redo, and adjustable text size.
• Keep your work protected with autosave and help resolving conflicting edits.
• Find problems faster with clearer error messages and a console for printed output.
• Run your sketches repeatedly with improved reliability.

Easier project management
• Import complete projects or choose individual sample projects to try again.
• Rename and duplicate projects and files.
• Open project folders from Files, including supported iCloud Drive locations, and share whole projects.

Learn and explore
• Browse a searchable offline reference with examples you can copy.
• Add compatible JavaScript libraries to p5.js projects and choose their load order.
• Enjoy expanded Processing compatibility, including StringList, circle, and square.

The old Add to Home Screen feature has been removed because it no longer works reliably.
